# Getting help with AXEM-SX

AXEM-SX is a small project. The support model is shaped to match.

## Before you reach out

The [documentation](https://docs.axem-sx.org/) covers most
questions. The search bar at the top of every page reaches all of
it, in English and in French.

## Where to ask

For now, two channels — one public, one private — and they cover
everything.

1. **Bugs, questions, and ideas** → [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DonX/axem-sx-website/issues)
   Public, searchable, async. One queue keeps things simple while
   the project is small. For a bug, please include the AXEM-SX
   version, what you tried, what happened, and what you expected.
   For a question or an idea, just say so in the title — we read
   them, and we rarely promise.

2. **Anything more personal** → `goldaxem@gmail.com`

As the project grows, we may add dedicated channels for questions
and ideas. We will not add them before we can sustain them.

## What to expect

Days, not hours. This is a workshop, not a help desk.

English and French are both welcome.

Patience and clarity get faster answers than urgency.
