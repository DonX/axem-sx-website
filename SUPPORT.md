# Getting help with AXEM-SX

AXEM-SX is a small project. The support model is shaped to match.

## Before you reach out

The [documentation](https://docs.axem-sx.org/) covers most
questions. The search bar at the top of every page reaches all of
it, in English and in French.

## Where to ask

The order matters — going in this order saves everyone time.

1. **A question** → [GitHub Discussions › Q&A](https://github.com/DonX/axem-sx-website/discussions/categories/q-a)
   Public, searchable, async. Other users with the same question
   benefit from your asking.

2. **A bug** → [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DonX/axem-sx-website/issues)
   Please include: AXEM-SX version, what you tried, what happened,
   what you expected.

3. **An idea** → [GitHub Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/DonX/axem-sx-website/discussions/categories/ideas)
   No promises and no roadmap. Good ideas get acknowledged.

4. **Anything else** → `goldaxem@gmail.com`

## What to expect

Days, not hours. This is a workshop, not a help desk.

English and French are both welcome.

Patience and clarity get faster answers than urgency.
